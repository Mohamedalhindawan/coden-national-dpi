# CODEN Reusable Visual Components

Status: draft foundation.

## Page chrome
- Header: lighthouse + Coden wordmark (approved asset only), section label, thin divider.
- Footer: document title/version, page number, short positioning line.
- Cover: Aden/Yemen image where appropriate, brand lockup, title, subtitle, version.

## Content components
- Section opener: numbered section, clear title, one-sentence purpose.
- Information card: short heading, concise body, optional icon.
- KPI card: metric name, value, unit, time period, source/status.
- Table: clear header, restrained rules, consistent alignment and units.
- Callout: purpose-specific note, warning, decision, or assumption.
- Diagram frame: figure number, title, scope, legend, diagram, notes/source.
- Timeline: phase, time range, deliverables, dependencies, approval gate.

## Diagram conventions
- Navy: governance, trust boundaries, primary control layers.
- Blue: platforms, services, technology components.
- Turquoise: data/service flows or approved emphasis.
- Grey: external or supporting systems.
- Red/amber/green: reserve for actual risk/status meaning; never decorative.
- Use labeled arrows and explicit boundaries. Color never carries meaning alone.

## Dashboard conventions
- Group by decision domain.
- Show metric definition, unit, period, and provenance.
- Distinguish actual, target, forecast, and illustrative data.
- Avoid fake live indicators. Mockups must say “Concept UI” or “Illustrative data”.

## Do not
- Stretch or recolor the logo.
- mix unrelated icon styles.
- use excessive gradients, shadows, or ornamental shapes.
- place tiny text in dense diagrams.
- present proposed integrations or future-state components as implemented.
