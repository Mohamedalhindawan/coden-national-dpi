# Figure 01 — CODEN Enterprise Architecture

**Status:** Target-state conceptual architecture (not a statement of current implementation).

## Purpose
Show how CODEN's governance model, four enterprise engines, shared digital foundations, products, and external ecosystem fit together as one operating and engineering system.

## Architecture layers
1. **Governance & human oversight:** strategy, architecture principles, portfolio priorities, risk ownership, accountable business/technical owners, and approval gates.
2. **AI Enterprise Control Plane:** agent registry and identity, orchestration/routing, policy enforcement, evaluation/observability, human-in-the-loop gates, and audit trail.
3. **Four enterprise engines:**
   - AI Enterprise OS — operating model, portfolio, workforce, policies, KPIs.
   - AI Engineering Factory — discover, specify, build, test, secure, release.
   - Continuous Improvement — telemetry, feedback, incidents, experiments, learning loops.
   - Knowledge & Intelligence — enterprise knowledge, standards, DPG/DPI registry, search, evidence.
4. **Shared digital platform:** identity/access, API/integration, data/interoperability, payments/messaging, DevSecOps/CI/CD, cloud/runtime, security/privacy, observability/resilience.
5. **Products and ecosystem:** TAMAM and reusable government/sector components connect to government entities, SFD and partners, DPGs, donors, and private sector participants.

## Key interpretation
- The diagram describes a **proposed target operating model**.
- Arrows represent intended governance and capability relationships, not verified live integrations.
- Product examples and external ecosystem participants are architectural scope categories, not confirmed deployments or signed partnerships.
- CODEN must retain human accountability for high-impact, security-sensitive, financial, legal, and public-service decisions.
