# CODEN Typography & Language Rules

Status: draft. Final font families must be selected after confirming availability, licensing, and Arabic glyph quality.

## Type hierarchy
- Display / cover title: strong, concise, high contrast.
- H1: section title.
- H2: subsection title.
- H3: component or diagram title.
- Body: comfortable reading size and line length.
- Caption: source, note, figure number, or metadata.
- Code: monospaced and visually distinct.

## Language direction
- Arabic paragraphs and tables: RTL.
- English paragraphs and technical identifiers: LTR.
- Mixed Arabic/English lines: isolate technical identifiers where needed; verify punctuation, parentheses, numerals, and code.
- Do not force Arabic text into an unsuitable Latin font or use a font with incomplete glyph coverage.

## Selection criteria
1. Readable Arabic and Latin glyphs.
2. Licensed for intended embedding and distribution.
3. Available consistently in authoring and PDF export environments.
4. Multiple weights where needed.
5. Stable rendering on Android, desktop, and print.

## Layout
Use a consistent type scale and avoid excessive font sizes or weights. Keep body text readable at actual A4 size. Never use image-generated text for final document copy.
