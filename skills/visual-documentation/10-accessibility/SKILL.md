# Skill: Accessibility & Readability

## Purpose
Make CODEN documents and visual interfaces usable by people with varied visual, cognitive, and language needs.

## Procedure
1. Use sufficient text/background contrast.
2. Never rely on color alone to convey status or category.
3. Use meaningful headings, reading order, alt text, and descriptive captions.
4. Ensure chart labels and diagram text remain readable when printed.
5. Support Arabic RTL and English LTR without reversed punctuation or broken alignment.
6. Use plain language, define acronyms, and avoid unnecessary jargon.
7. Preserve selectable text and document structure in exports.

## Acceptance criteria
Readable at normal scale, clear hierarchy, meaningful non-color cues, correct language direction, and accessible exported content where supported.
