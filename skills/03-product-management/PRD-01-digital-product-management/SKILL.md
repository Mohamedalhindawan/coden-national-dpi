# PRD-01 — Digital Product Management

> Status: Draft | Version: 1.0.0 | Owner: Product Lead

## Purpose
Ensure CODEN builds products that solve validated user problems and produce measurable outcomes.

## Scope
Product strategy, discovery, roadmaps, prioritization, requirements, release planning, adoption, and product lifecycle.

## Required inputs
Problem statement; user groups; service context; constraints; available evidence; sponsor and product owner; budget envelope; risk and data classification.

## Workflow
1. Write a problem statement and identify affected user groups.
2. Conduct discovery using interviews, observation, service data, and existing research where appropriate.
3. Map current and target user journeys, including low-connectivity and accessibility needs.
4. Define product outcomes, baseline metrics, assumptions, and non-goals.
5. Prioritize using user impact, urgency, feasibility, risk, cost, and strategic fit.
6. Produce a PRD with user stories and testable acceptance criteria.
7. Validate scope with engineering, architecture, security, privacy, and operations.
8. Release incrementally; measure adoption, task success, support burden, and outcomes.
9. Maintain feedback loops and explicit deprecation/retirement plans.

## Deliverables
Product brief; personas or user-group profiles; journey maps; PRD; prioritized backlog; release plan; outcome dashboard; product decision log.

## Quality gates
Every initiative has a named product owner, target users, validated problem, measurable outcome, acceptance criteria, support model, and an explicit MVP boundary.

## Controls
Do not claim user validation without evidence. Do not design eligibility, payment, identity, or health decisions as autonomous AI decisions. Obtain appropriate human and institutional approval for high-impact workflows.

## Evaluation
Measure user task completion, adoption, accessibility, reliability, time-to-value, cost per successful transaction, and unresolved user needs.

## Security
Classify product data before collection; minimize personal data; define retention and access rules with security and privacy owners. Do not use real citizen records in prototype discovery or test fixtures. Treat user research and analytics as restricted project data.

## Failure and escalation
Pause prioritization or release when user evidence is insufficient, acceptance criteria are ambiguous, safety/accessibility issues remain unresolved, or a proposed feature affects eligibility, payments, identity, health, or legal rights without accountable institutional approval. Record the decision and escalate to the product sponsor and relevant control owners.
