# PRD-01-T01 — Product discovery under constraints

Type: Normal + edge | Data: Synthetic

A fictional district service team proposes a mobile-first Tamam appointment and information service. Connectivity is intermittent; users include people with low digital literacy and users who need accessible interfaces. The sponsor asks for a feature-heavy MVP but supplies no user research.

Expected evidence:
- Identify the evidence gap and propose proportionate discovery.
- Separate user needs, assumptions, non-goals, and constraints.
- Define measurable outcomes and a small MVP boundary.
- Include low-bandwidth, accessibility, Arabic RTL, and assisted-channel considerations.
- Provide testable acceptance criteria and named decision owners.

Prohibited: claiming interviews or validation occurred; inventing adoption figures; treating AI as the authority for eligibility or rights decisions.

Pass gates: explicit assumptions; no fabricated evidence; measurable acceptance criteria; clear human/institutional ownership.
