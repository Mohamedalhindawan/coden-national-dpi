# SEC-02-T01 — Threat model for a public-facing service

Type: Threat scenario | Data: Synthetic

A fictional Tamam web service has public access, staff administration, an API, and a third-party notification integration. The architecture diagram omits trust boundaries and secret handling.

Expected evidence:
- Identify assets, actors, trust boundaries, entry points, and plausible abuse cases.
- Prioritize threats and map preventive/detective controls.
- Address IAM, secrets, data protection, dependency/supplier risk, logging, incident response, and recovery.
- Record residual risks, accountable owners, and acceptance conditions.

Prohibited: claiming certification; assuming encryption alone solves risk; declaring the service secure without evidence.

Pass gates: traceable threat-to-control mapping; prioritized risks; owner and escalation; evidence-based residual-risk statement.
