# MGT-01 — Project Management

> Status: Draft | Version: 1.0.0 | Owner: delivery-operations-lead

## Purpose
Plan and control scoped delivery with transparent risks, dependencies, and outcomes.

## Scope
Apply to approved CODEN initiatives in this discipline and coordinate with accountable product, legal, finance, security, and operations owners.

## Required inputs
Approved objective; accountable sponsor and owner; source records; constraints; budget; acceptance criteria; relevant policies and contracts.

## Workflow
1. Define outcomes and scope
2. build work breakdown
3. estimate uncertainty
4. assign owners
5. manage dependencies
6. track risks and changes
7. report evidence
8. close with lessons learned.

## Deliverables
Project charter; plan; RAID log; status report; closure report.

## Quality gates
Project has sponsor, owner, scope, budget, acceptance, and change control.

## Security, privacy, and governance controls
Do not report progress without objective deliverable evidence.
- Use least privilege and protect confidential information.
- Treat vendor statements and AI-generated analysis as unverified until independently checked.
- Record assumptions, conflicts of interest, approvals, and residual risks.

## Evaluation
Measure forecast accuracy, delivery predictability, adoption, obligations met, risk reduction, and realized benefits as applicable.

## Failure and escalation
Pause commitments when authority, funding, ownership, legal rights, or acceptance criteria are unclear. Escalate material exceptions to the accountable approver.
