# Enterprise risk register contract

This record captures an identified risk associated with an initiative, its likelihood and impact ratings, current lifecycle status, and—when mitigation is underway or the risk is accepted/closed—the accountable owner and mitigation plan.

## Rating model

Likelihood and impact are independent ordinal ratings from 1 to 5. This contract does not prescribe universal definitions for each rating, calculate a risk appetite threshold, or determine whether a risk is acceptable. Those scales and escalation thresholds must be approved in organizational policy.

## Governance boundaries

- Mitigating, accepted, and closed risks require a named risk owner and mitigation plan.
- A closed risk requires a review timestamp.
- The schema does not authenticate the owner, reviewer, or approver.
- Risk acceptance is not a substitute for legal, security, privacy, or financial authorization.
- Cross-record initiative validation, review cadence, escalation, and immutable history belong in trusted application services.

## Scope

Synthetic fixture only. No risk scoring engine, alerting, control testing, incident workflow, or live portfolio reporting is implemented. Do not place secrets or personal data in risk descriptions.
