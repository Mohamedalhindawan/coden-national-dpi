# CODEN National Digital Public Infrastructure

## Project status
This repository documents a proposed, phased national Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) initiative for Yemen. It is a proposal and prototype plan, not an approved or operational government platform.

## Vision
Build reusable, secure, open-source digital foundations that help people access public services and social support more easily.

## Proposed operating model
- **SFD:** potential institutional host, sponsor, and partner, subject to approval.
- **YDS (Yemen Digital Service):** proposed coordination unit for strategy, standards, partnerships, and delivery oversight.
- **CODEN:** proposed technical implementation company and open-source delivery partner.
- **Tamam:** working name for a citizen-facing super-app; brand and domain availability are not yet verified.

## Initial capability areas
1. Digital identity and authentication, subject to legal authority and safeguards.
2. Payments and social-assistance delivery, in coordination with authorized financial institutions.
3. Secure interoperability and data exchange, with X-Road under evaluation.
4. Public-service access and case/status tracking.
5. Social-protection eligibility support with privacy, auditability, and human review.
6. VSLA and community-finance workflows where appropriate.

## Principles
- Start small, measure results, and scale only after evidence of value.
- Prefer open standards and maintained open-source components where suitable.
- Minimize personal data and enforce role-based access, consent/legal basis, logging, and retention rules.
- Require human approval for high-impact identity, financial, security, and legal decisions.
- Do not treat AI-generated decisions as final eligibility or payment determinations.
- Threat-model, test, document, and independently review each production release.

## Suggested first milestone
Create a non-production prototype for one SFD social-protection workflow using synthetic data only. Define users, process maps, data fields, authorization rules, audit events, success measures, and a test plan before integrating real services or personal data.

## Governance note
Any proposed ownership, institutional mandate, data-sharing arrangement, and financial-service role must be reviewed and formally approved by the relevant Yemeni authorities and partners before implementation.
