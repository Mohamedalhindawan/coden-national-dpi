# CODEN Decision Rights — Draft for Human Approval

**Status:** Proposed, not legally authorized. Confirm actual roles and delegated authority before use.

| Decision | Required approver | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Source-code PR with low impact | Authorized code reviewer | Approved PR and CI run |
| Identity, payments or eligibility rules | Legally authorized domain owner plus security/privacy review | Signed decision record |
| Production deployment | Authorized service owner and release approver | Change ticket and readiness evidence |
| Spending and procurement | Delegated financial authority | Approved purchase/contract |
| Agent permission expansion | Security owner and authorized system owner | Access decision and audit log |
| Public fees, government data, official integration | Relevant public authority and legal review | Formal authorization |

CODEN, YDS and SFD roles must not be conflated; YDS/SFD arrangements remain proposals until formally approved.
