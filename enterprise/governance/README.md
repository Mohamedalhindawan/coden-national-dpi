# CODEN governance foundation

**Status:** Draft templates only. Governance is not enforced merely by committing Markdown/YAML.

## Institutional boundaries
- Government: legal and regulatory authority, official data stewardship and public decisions.
- SFD: potential development and financing role only within authorized mandates.
- YDS: proposed coordination model, subject to formal approval.
- CODEN: technical engineering/delivery under an authorized agreement.

## Controls requiring human approval
Identity, payment, eligibility, financial spending, production deployments, access escalation, security-policy changes, public charges and binding contracts.

## Required next steps
Approve decision rights and RACI; appoint accountable owners; configure repository branch protection; establish auditable review and exception process.
