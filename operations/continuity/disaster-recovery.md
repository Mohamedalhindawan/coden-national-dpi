# CODEN Continuity and Disaster Recovery — Draft

**Status:** Planning template; no tested backups or disaster recovery capability is asserted.

For each future production service record: service owner, critical dependencies, data classification, RTO, RPO, backup method, encryption and key custody, restore test date, failover procedure, contact tree, incident commander, and signed approval.

## Acceptance gate
Do not mark a service production-ready until a restore test and responsible human approval are evidenced. Keep synthetic prototype and future production environments clearly separate.
