# TAMAM — Product Charter (Draft)

**Reference:** CODEN Playbook V4.0 §§40–41. **Status:** Concept/prototype only.

## Purpose
Arabic RTL, mobile-first, low-bandwidth citizen-facing access to future authorized digital services. The current repository contains only a synthetic social-support browser demo.

## Candidate modules
Social Support, Takafol, for Business, Marketplace, Mini Apps, Government Services. These are planned concepts, not deployed modules.

## Boundaries
No real citizen records, official identity verification, government decisions, payments, eligibility determinations or official integration in the existing prototype.

## Product gates
User research and authorized scope; PRD and acceptance; privacy and threat modeling; accessibility; API contracts; synthetic integration tests; legal authorization; pilot acceptance; operations and support readiness.
